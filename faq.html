❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mr Coin Wala

How do I buy coins on Mr Coin Wala? ⌄
Browse the Buy Coins or Bulk Coins pages, use filters to narrow down by category or price, add items to your cart, and complete checkout with your delivery address.
How do I sell my coins? ⌄
Create an account, go to the Sell Product page, upload clear photos, fill in quantity, price, and location details, and submit. Your listing goes live instantly on your Seller Dashboard.
Is there a commission fee for sellers? ⌄
We charge a small service fee only on completed transactions. Listing a product is always free.
How are delivery timelines calculated? ⌄
Delivery estimates are based on the seller's district and order size — single pieces ship faster than large bulk orders.
Can I track my order status? ⌄
Yes, visit the My Orders page anytime to see real-time status updates from Confirmed to Delivered.
What bulk coin categories are supported? ⌄
We support paddy husk, rice bran, wheat straw, bagasse, corn cob and husk, mustard cake, groundnut shell, coconut shell and fiber, cotton waste, saw dust, wood chips, and bamboo waste.
How do I contact customer support? ⌄
Use the floating chat button, or visit our Contact page to call, email, or send us a message directly.
