🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery addresses, and listing details when you register, buy, or sell on Mr Coin Wala. We also store your cart, wishlist, and order history locally in your browser to power core shopping features.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, connect buyers with sellers, personalize product recommendations, provide customer support, and improve our platform. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

In this version of the application, account, cart, wishlist, order, and listing data are stored securely in your device's local browser storage. When connected to our backend infrastructure, this data is stored on encrypted servers with access controls.

4. Sharing of Information

We share only the information necessary to complete a transaction — for example, a buyer's delivery address is shared with the relevant seller to fulfil an order.

5. Your Rights

You may access, update, or request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting our support team or through your profile settings.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage technologies to keep you logged in, remember your cart and wishlist, and improve site performance.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out via our Contact page.
