ℹ️ About Mr Coin Wala

Connecting coin makers directly to buyers, nationwide

Mr Coin Wala is a dedicated online marketplace for handcrafted iron coins and gifting coins. We connect makers directly with buyers — removing unnecessary middlemen and helping both sides get a fairer deal.

Our Mission

To help every coin maker, however small, reach verified buyers across the country with simple listing and transparent pricing.

What We Offer

  • A curated marketplace for 10+ coin categories, including bulk/wholesale coin orders.
  • Verified seller badges and district/state-level delivery estimates.
  • Daily market price tracking to help you time your sale or purchase.
  • Simple listing tools so any seller can start selling in minutes.

Why Bulk Coins Matter

Weddings, festivals and corporate events often need coins in bulk quantity. Mr Coin Wala creates a direct channel between coin makers and bulk buyers — helping sellers get more consistent orders.
